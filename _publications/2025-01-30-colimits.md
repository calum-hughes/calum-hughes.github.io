We show that for an extensive $1$-category $\mathcal{E}$ with pullback-stable coequalisers admitting free internal categories over internal graphs, the $2$-category $\mathbf{Cat}(\mathcal{E})$ of internal categories, functors and natural transformations has finite $2$-colimits. In addition, $\mathbf{Cat}(\mathcal{E})$ is extensive and codescent coequalisers are stable under pullback along discrete Conduché fibrations. Moreover, we give converse results to this.
